About

What?

This website is an inflation tracker, utilising publicly available data to model the rise of prices in Austria over the last decade, as well as the loss of purchasing power of the Austrian consumer in various key areas.

Why?

Inflation remains a major issue in most EU countries, especially Austria. One of the website's goals is to show how "raw" government data can be transformed into a tool of public interest. The tool itself is non-partisan.

How?

This page uses openly available information from the Austrian government to model the change in prices and corresponding graphs. The site uses the Consumer Price Index (CPI) as its benchmark of inflation. It was built with Python, Flask, SQLite for data management and Plotly.js for interactive visualisations.

The top inflation driver is the basket category that added the most percentage points to last year's inflation. This depends both on how much its prices rose and on how much of the average household's spending it makes up.

Data source: Statistik Austria - data.statistik.gv.at (CC BY 4.0)
